# SendIt style guide

SendIt follows the shared ClockIt / ManageIt component system, with a blue and
cream brand palette. Shared status colors, spacing, typography, buttons, cards,
forms, and navigation retain their common meanings and proportions.

## Brand tokens

- Cream canvas: `#faf4e9`
- Warm cream surface: `#fffcf5`
- Deep blue text and brand: `#203b59`
- Muted blue: `#b7c9d8`
- SendIt blue accent: `#2f6b9a`

The app overrides the semantic tokens in `frontend/src/styles/_tokens.scss`.
Use those tokens rather than hard-coding brand colors in components.
